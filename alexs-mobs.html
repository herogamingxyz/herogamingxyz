Alex's Mobs

Alex's Mobs is one of the most popular creature expansion mods for Minecraft. This mod adds over 80 unique animals, fantasy creatures, and realistic mobs to the game while maintaining the vanilla Minecraft style. From powerful Grizzly Bears and Crocodiles to exotic creatures like Komodo Dragons, Orcas, and Soul Vultures, every mob has unique behaviors, drops, and interactions. The mod focuses on making Minecraft's world feel more alive and immersive without breaking the original gameplay experience. Every biome becomes more exciting to explore thanks to the huge variety of creatures that naturally spawn throughout the world. Whether you enjoy exploration, adventure, survival, or simply discovering new wildlife, Alex's Mobs provides hundreds of hours of additional content and greatly improves the overall Minecraft experience.

Installation Guide

1. Install Minecraft Forge or NeoForge for your game version. 2. Download the latest version of Alex's Mobs. 3. Download the required Citadel library mod. 4. Move both files into your Minecraft Mods folder. 5. Launch Minecraft using the Forge profile. 6. Create or load a world and start exploring new creatures.
