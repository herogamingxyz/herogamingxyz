Better Combat

Better Combat completely transforms Minecraft's combat system with smooth attack animations, combo-based fighting, improved weapon mechanics, and immersive third-person combat. The mod supports swords, axes, spears, daggers, and many other weapon types while remaining compatible with many popular RPG and adventure mods.

Installation Guide

1. Install Fabric Loader for your Minecraft version. 2. Download Better Combat and its required dependencies. 3. Move all downloaded files into the "mods" folder. 4. Launch Minecraft using the Fabric profile. 5. Enjoy a completely new combat experience with improved animations.
