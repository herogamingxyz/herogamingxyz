Working TV Addon

The Working TV Addon adds modern televisions to Minecraft Bedrock Edition, making your houses, apartments, and cities look more realistic. The addon includes multiple TV designs, decorative furniture, and interactive features to improve your building experience. Whether you're building a luxury mansion, modern apartment, gaming room, or city map, this addon provides stylish television models that perfectly fit your interior designs. It is lightweight, multiplayer compatible, and supports the latest Minecraft Bedrock versions. Features: • Multiple TV designs • Modern decoration blocks • Perfect for houses and cities • Survival & Creative compatible • Multiplayer support • Lightweight addon • Easy installation • Supports latest Minecraft Bedrock Edition

Installation Guide

1. Download the Working TV Addon from the link below. 2. Open the downloaded .mcpack or .mcaddon file with Minecraft. 3. Wait for the addon to import successfully. 4. Create a new world or edit an existing one. 5. Enable the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack. 6. Turn on Experimental Features if required. 7. Enter your world and enjoy decorating with working TVs.
