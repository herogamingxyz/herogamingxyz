Immersive Aircraft

Immersive Aircraft is a Minecraft mod that adds realistic and easy-to-use aircraft to the game. Players can craft and fly different vehicles such as biplanes, gyrocopters, airships, and cargo aircraft to explore the world much faster. The mod features smooth controls, multiplayer support, and a vanilla-friendly design, making long-distance travel more fun and immersive.

Installation Guide

1. Install Minecraft Fabric or Forge (depending on the supported version). 2. Download the Immersive Aircraft mod. 3. Place the mod file into your Minecraft "mods" folder. 4. Launch Minecraft using the correct mod loader profile. 5. Craft your first aircraft and start exploring the skies.
